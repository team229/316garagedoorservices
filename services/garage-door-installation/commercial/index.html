What We Install

Doors chosen for the way you work.

A commercial door cycles dozens of times a day. That changes the door, the springs, the track and the opener we recommend.

New installation

Building out a new unit, adding a loading bay or converting space? We handle it end to end: measuring the opening, supplying the door, setting the tracks and springs, mounting the opener and testing the whole system before we leave.

Sectional doors

The most common choice for warehouses, shops and service bays. They open vertically and sit against the ceiling, so they take up very little interior room and keep the apron outside clear.

Insulated doors

If your bay is heated or you store temperature-sensitive stock, insulation matters on a cold Fraser Valley morning. Insulated panels are also quieter and stand up better to carts and forklifts.

Steel, aluminum and glass

  • Steel is strong, secure and cost-effective, a good default for docks and industrial units.
  • Aluminum and glass suit restaurants, dealerships, retail fronts and showrooms where daylight and visibility matter.

Rolling steel and roll-up doors

These coil above the opening, which suits tight spaces and high-security applications. Common on storage units, service entrances and buildings with limited headroom.

Replacement

Replacing a worn or damaged door is often smarter than paying for the same repair twice. We remove the old door, check the opening and framing, and install the new door and hardware so it runs properly from day one. Not sure whether to repair or replace? We will tell you honestly. Our habit is to diagnose the real cause rather than swap parts until the door moves.

Openers & Hardware

A door is only as good as what runs it.

The most common expensive mistake we see is a light-duty opener on a heavy commercial door.

Openers

We install and set up LiftMaster and myQ openers, including smart-enabled models, with safety sensors aligned and tested. The opener has to match the door's weight, size and usage. Put a residential-grade unit on a dock door and you will be replacing it again before long.

Springs, cables and track

Springs do the heavy lifting, and on commercial doors we size them for the door's weight. Where a door opens many times a day, we can quote high-cycle springs that last far longer under that load. Spring work carries a one-year warranty.

We also replace cables, rollers and tracks as part of an installation, so you are not pairing a new door with tired old hardware.

Before You Choose

What to weigh up first.

The same questions come up on nearly every commercial job.

Cycle count and weight

A bay used all day needs springs and an opener rated for that load. Getting the spring cycle rating right up front saves you from emergency calls later.

Insulation and sealing

Older doors are cold and drafty. Modern insulated doors with proper seals keep heat in and keep dust and moisture out.

Security and safety

Commercial doors are a main entry point. We make sure the door, locks where applicable and safety sensors are all set up so the door stops when it should.

Appearance

A door is one of the first things customers see. Panel style, colour and glass can be matched to the building without giving up durability.

Warning Signs

When replacement beats another repair.

If any of these are familiar, it may be time to look at a new door rather than another patch.

  • A door that is noisy, jerky or off-track
  • Broken or repeatedly failing springs and cables
  • Dented, cracked or delaminating panels
  • An opener that struggles, overheats or stops mid-travel
  • Safety sensors that keep misaligning
  • High heating costs from an uninsulated door

A door that fails often usually has an underlying cause: the wrong springs, an undersized opener, or worn tracks. We look for that cause first. Regular attention keeps a new door running, and our inspections cover springs, cables, rollers, tracks, hinges, sensors and opener settings. If a door does go down unexpectedly, our commercial repair service runs 24/7.

Common Questions

Commercial garage door installation FAQs.

Still unsure? Call 604-388-9868 and we will point you the right way.

How much does commercial garage door installation cost?

It depends on the door size, material, insulation level, opener and whether we are replacing an existing door. We provide a free consultation and a written price you approve before work begins, so you know the cost up front.

How long does a commercial garage door installation take?

Most single-door installs are completed in a day, though larger or custom doors take longer. We give you a time estimate during the consultation and plan around your business hours.

Should I repair or replace my commercial garage door?

If the door is structurally sound and one part failed, repair makes sense. If panels are damaged, the door keeps breaking down, or it is badly out of balance, replacement is usually better value. We will recommend honestly either way.

Do you install insulated commercial garage doors?

Yes. We install insulated sectional doors as well as steel, aluminum and glass options, depending on what your building needs.

Do you install and service commercial openers?

Yes. We install LiftMaster and myQ openers and can repair or replace springs, cables, sensors and other components as part of the job.

Can I get emergency commercial service outside normal hours?

Yes. Commercial service runs 24/7 across our service area. Residential hours are 9 AM to 6 PM.
