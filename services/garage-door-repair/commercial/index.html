What We See

The failures that stop a business.

Commercial doors cycle far more than a home door, so they fail in fairly predictable ways. Knowing the cause helps us fix it fast.

  • Broken springs. The most common reason a heavy door suddenly will not lift.
  • Snapped cables. The door drops on one side or hangs crooked.
  • Bent tracks. Often the result of a forklift or a delivery truck clipping the door.
  • Damaged panels, worn rollers and hinges. The usual result of daily heavy use.
  • Opener faults. Motors, gears and boards give out eventually under constant use.
  • Safety sensors. Knocked out of line, they make the door reverse or refuse to close.

We find the real fault, explain it in plain language, and give you the price before we touch anything.

What We Offer

Everything a busy door needs.

One team for emergency repair, springs, openers, replacement and scheduled maintenance.

Emergency commercial repair

A door stuck open overnight or jammed shut on a delivery morning cannot wait, so we answer commercial calls around the clock. We secure the building first, then fix the door. If a part has to be ordered, we make the opening safe and locked down in the meantime.

Spring and cable repair

Commercial doors are heavier and cycle many more times a day, so their springs wear out faster. We replace torsion and extension springs and can fit high-cycle springs where the usage justifies them. Spring work carries a one-year warranty. This is not a job for in-house staff to attempt, because the stored tension is dangerous.

Opener repair and installation

We repair and install commercial-grade openers, including LiftMaster and myQ models, along with keypads, remotes and safety sensors. If your opener is noisy, stopping halfway or ignoring remotes, we will tell you honestly whether it is a repair or a replacement.

Installation and replacement

Sometimes a new door is the right call. We install commercial sectional doors, insulated steel doors and rolling steel doors for warehouses, auto shops, dealerships, strata parkades, restaurants and loading docks. See commercial garage door installation.

Maintenance and inspection

A scheduled tune-up costs less than an emergency call. We check springs, cables, rollers, tracks, hinges, the opener and the sensors, then lubricate and adjust what needs it. For businesses that rely on their doors every day, we can set up a regular visit so problems get caught before they shut you down.

Repair or Replace

How to make the call.

Repair is usually right when the panels and tracks are sound and one part has failed.

Replacement tends to be the better value when:

  • The panels are badly dented, rusted or cracked and no longer seal
  • The same door has needed repeat repairs within a year or two
  • The door is poorly insulated and your heating bill shows it
  • Parts are hard to find for an older model
  • Security or appearance matters for a customer-facing building

When it is close, we will give you both numbers so you can decide with the real figures in front of you.
