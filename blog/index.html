When To Call

Reading is good. Fixing is better.

Some problems are safe to live with for a few days. Some are not.

If you hear a loud bang, the door will not lift, or the opener hums without moving the door, stop using it and call us. A broken spring leaves the door very heavy, and forcing it can bend tracks and burn out the opener, turning one repair into several.

Squeaks, slow movement, a door that reverses on its own, or a bottom seal that has gone hard are all smaller things worth sorting before they get worse. Either way, the first step is a free consultation, and you approve the price before any work starts.

Can I repair a garage door myself?

Some things, like lubricating rollers or cleaning safety sensors, are fine to do yourself. Spring, cable and track work is dangerous because of the stored tension, so it is best left to a technician.

How do I know when to call a professional?

If the door will not open or close, makes a loud bang or grinding noise, reverses on its own, or hangs crooked, call us. It is better to catch it early than to force a damaged door.
