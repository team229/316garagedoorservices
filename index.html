Garage Door Repair Questions

Garage door repair FAQs.

Straight answers about cost, timing and coverage in the Lower Mainland. Still unsure? Call 604-388-9868.

How much does garage door repair cost in Langley BC?

Cost depends on door size, parts and how long the fault has been left. Cables, rollers, hinges and bottom seals are the most economical jobs; torsion or extension spring replacement and opener installation cost more because they use higher-rated parts and more labour. Call 604-388-9868 and describe the symptom — you get a free consultation and a price before work starts.

How fast can you get to my garage door?

We offer same-day and weekend appointments in Langley and across the Lower Mainland when the schedule allows, plus Sunday emergency call-outs. Most Langley jobs are booked within a few hours of your call.

Do you replace broken torsion and extension springs?

Yes. We replace both torsion and extension springs and balance the door so the opener is not lifting weight it was never designed for. Replacing springs without balancing the door is a common cause of premature opener failure.

Are you a LiftMaster authorized installer?

Yes. We install LiftMaster openers, including quiet belt-drive motors that link to the myQ app so you can open, close and check your garage door status from your phone.

Why does my garage door reverse when closing?

Usually photo eyes out of alignment, a dirty or blocked safety sensor lens, an obstruction in the door path, or opener force settings that have drifted out of specification. We realign and clean the sensors and reset the opener limits and force settings.

Do you install brand new garage doors?

Yes. We install new residential garage doors for renovations, new builds and replacements, matched to your opening and the style of your house, with hardware and finish options to suit your budget.

Which areas of the Lower Mainland do you serve?

We are based in Langley and serve Langley, Surrey, White Rock, Burnaby, Coquitlam, Maple Ridge, Vancouver, Richmond, Abbotsford, Delta and surrounding communities. Call to confirm availability in your area.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes. Consultations are free and you approve the price before any work begins — no surprise call-out fees and no obligation to proceed.
