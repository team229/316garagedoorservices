Our Coverage

One team, the whole Lower Mainland.

Same parts, same warranty and the same people, whether you are in Langley or Chilliwack.

We keep our service area honest. If we say we cover a city on this page, we actually go there, and we do not charge a travel fee to do it. That matters when a job is a bit further out, because some companies will either turn you down or add a fuel charge.

Our main base is Langley, which is why the Fraser Valley and the Tri-Cities tend to get our fastest response. The North Shore and Vancouver are still a normal part of the week, particularly for spring replacements and new door installations.

Service area FAQs.

Still unsure? Call 604-388-9868 and we will point you the right way.

Which cities do you serve?

We serve Langley, Surrey, White Rock, Burnaby, Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam, Port Moody, Maple Ridge, Vancouver, North Vancouver, West Vancouver, Richmond, Abbotsford, Chilliwack, Delta, New Westminster and Pitt Meadows, and the surrounding areas. If you are just outside those cities, call 604-388-9868 and we will tell you whether we can get to you.

Do you charge a travel fee?

No. There are no travel fees anywhere in our Lower Mainland service area, so the price you approve is the price you pay.

Are you based in Langley?

Yes. We are a family-owned company based in Langley, BC, and we have been servicing the Lower Mainland since 2015.

Can I get same-day garage door repair?

Often, yes, depending on the schedule and how far out you are. Call early in the day for the best chance of a same-day visit.

Do you serve commercial properties too?

Yes. We repair and install commercial garage doors across the service area, with 24/7 emergency service, while residential hours are 9 AM to 6 PM.
