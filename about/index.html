Our Story

Built on doing it right the first time.

No call centre, no rotating crew, no runaround. Just a local tradesperson who knows garage doors.

316 Garage Door Services started in Langley in 2015 with a simple standard: diagnose the real cause, explain it in plain language, and fix it properly the first time. Garage doors are the heaviest moving part of a home, and they carry stored spring tension that can cause serious injury. A door that is not repaired correctly is not just an inconvenience, it is a safety problem.

Over the years that standard has built a customer base that is mostly repeat business and referrals. Homeowners pass our name to neighbours. Property managers call us back. Small business owners bring us their shop and warehouse doors because we show up and we finish the job.

What we actually do all day

  • Broken torsion and extension spring replacement, with the door rebalanced afterwards
  • Cable, roller, hinge, track and panel repairs
  • LiftMaster and myQ opener repair, installation and programming
  • Safety sensor, weather seal and general door servicing
  • New residential and commercial garage door installation
Meet The Technician

You are hiring an expert, not a parts swap.

Most garage door problems have a cause that is one step back from the part that failed.

Anyone can swap a spring. The difference is knowing why it broke, and what else is close behind it. When we inspect a door, we check the springs, cables, drums, bearings, rollers, hinges, tracks, the opener and the safety sensors as one system. That is how we catch the worn bearing or the stretched cable that would have caused a second breakdown a month later.

We also tell you the truth about repair versus replacement. If a door has years of life left, we repair it. If you are pouring money into a door that is failing structurally, we will say so, and the decision is yours. No pressure, no upsell script.
